## The Pre-Upload Seller Central Compliance Checklist

Amazon's rules are famously strict. One wrong badge (like adding "Best Seller" or "Free Shipping" text to your main image) can get your listing suppressed instantly. Before you download your final assets from the generator, run them through this quick review workflow:

- [ ] **Main Image Purity:** Is the background strictly RGB(255,255,255) with no props, logos, or watermarks?
- [ ] **Frame Usage:** Does the product take up 85%+ of the longest side?
- [ ] **No Unsupported Claims:** Did you ensure your infographics are free of unsubstantiated medical claims, promotional text, or prohibited badges?
- [ ] **Resolution Minimums:** Are the images at least 1000 pixels on the longest side to enable the zoom feature?
